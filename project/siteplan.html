SPA Consultoria Integral

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

SPA Consultoria Integral

The name represents a Peruvian consulting company specialized in occupational safety, health, and environmental management.

The name was selected because it reflects the company's comprehensive consulting services and professional identity.

Proposed domain: spaconsig.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the SPA Consultoria Integral website is to introduce the company and provide information about its occupational safety, health, and environmental consulting services.

The website will have three main pages:

  • Home: Company introduction and client information.
  • Services: Information about consulting services and professional training.
  • Contact: Contact information and quotation request form.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent what potential clients may want to know when visiting the website.

  • What occupational safety and health services does SPA Consultoria Integral provide?
  • How can my company request a quotation for consulting services?
  • Does the company provide specialized occupational safety training?
  • How can I contact SPA Consultoria Integral?
